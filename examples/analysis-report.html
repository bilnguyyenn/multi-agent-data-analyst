Answer

On this generated dataset, the conversion model achieves holdout ROC AUC 0.906, compared with 0.500 for the naive baseline. A separate regression stress test reverses the target relationship in the final three time cohorts. Holdout RMSE rises to 5.822, worse than the baseline's 2.996. The engine surfaces that failure rather than presenting the model as useful. These are synthetic demonstrations of evaluation behavior, not evidence of real-world predictive accuracy or causal effects.
